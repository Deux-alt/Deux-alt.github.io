Where purchasing time goes

Every contractor buys a little differently, but the hours tend to disappear in the same places: the handoffs between one step and the next.

  1. Request
  2. Requests arrive by text, photo and phone call
  3. Quote
  4. Quote details get retyped into the order
  5. PO
  6. Orders wait on sign-off
  7. Approval
  8. Someone chases suppliers for confirmations
  9. Delivery
  10. Invoices get checked against orders by hand
  11. Invoice

How we work

We don't arrive with a finished product to sell you. We find out what's slowing your purchasing down, then fix the part that matters most.

  1. 1

    We learn how you buy today

    A short conversation about how requests come in, how orders get placed and where things slip.

  2. 2

    We test on your own documents

    Send a few sample requests or purchase orders, prices blacked out, and we show you what can be captured and checked automatically.

  3. 3

    We build only what saves time

    If it doesn't remove real hours or real mistakes, we don't build it. It's designed to work alongside the software you already use.

Your team keeps every decision. Nothing gets ordered or approved without a person signing off.

We also take on wider AI implementation and consulting work for contractors and service businesses.

About Deux

Deux is led by Demin Evans.

We're a small, focused agency working with commercial contractors on one problem: the admin that builds up around buying materials.

From the first call to the finished build, you're talking to the people doing the work.
